pixelforge starter font: 5x7 capitals, digits and punctuation. `#` is ink (see sprite.json "palette").
Each glyph is a "= X" line, then 7 rows of the same length (its width). Lowercase letters reuse the
capitals on export unless you draw them. Edit freely; `pixelforge tiles <font>` previews it.
